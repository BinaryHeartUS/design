# Slides and social

Slides and social posts use the print look (Archivo + Fira Code, cream and navy), but with far fewer words.

## Sizes

- An Instagram feed post is `1080 × 1350` (4:5), and a carousel uses the same size for every slide.
- An Instagram story is `1080 × 1920`.
    - Keep text out of the top 250px and bottom 300px, where the UI sits.
- A slide deck is `1920 × 1080` (16:9).
- Build them as HTML pages sized to the canvas, with embedded fonts and images, then export to PNG or PDF.
    - If the user wants .pptx, use the pptx skill with these tokens.

## Instagram

- Use one message per post.
    - The headline is Archivo 800 at 90-120px, and an eyebrow in Fira Code tracked caps sits over a 3px red bar.
- The event post is the poster's navy date panel, enlarged: the weekday in pink, the big date, the time, and the location.
    - One illustration goes on the cream area.
- In carousels, the first slide is the hook (the date or the headline).
    - Middle slides each give one detail, and the last slide has the call to action and handle, with the wordmark and heart logo.
- Stories use a navy full bleed, the big date in white, and a red "link" chip.
    - Leave room for the link sticker.
- Put `@binaryheartatnu` (or the chapter handle) on every graphic in Fira Code.
- The caption voice matches email: plain and warm, stating the date, time, and place, with no hashtag walls (three at most).

## Slide decks (callouts, training, partner pitches)

- The title slide is cream, with the chapter lockup at the top left, a two-line navy/red headline, the date, and an illustration on a navy tile.
- Section dividers are full navy, with the section name in white Archivo 800 and a Fira Code "01 / 02" counter only when the order matters.
- Content slides get at most one idea and five bullets, with the illustration on the right third.
    - Stats slides use Fira Code numbers with confirmed data only.
- The closing slide has the call to action, a QR code, the handle, and the email, above a navy footer band reading "Upcycle · Upskill · Uplift".
- Speaker notes carry the detail, so slides stay sparse.
