# Fall 2026 Subject Lines and Send Schedule

Pick one subject line per email, with the first option being the recommended one.
Each email's inbox preview text is already built into its HTML, so it shows up automatically after the subject line.

## Suggested send schedule (goal: turnout on Thursday, October 8)

- **Recruitment email:** send as soon as possible (this weekend, September 26-27).
    - It gives people 10+ days of notice to plan around the meeting.
- **Post call-out email (callout version only):** send the evening of the callout meeting.
- **First meeting announcement:** send Monday, October 5 or Tuesday, October 6.
    - Early in the week lands before people fill up their Thursday.
- **Event notification:** send the morning of Thursday, October 8 (around 8-9 AM).
    - A same-day reminder is the biggest single boost for drop-in turnout.
- **Welcome email:** send individually after someone joins, which works best within 24 hours of their first visit.
- **Event description:** this is the Cats on Campus event page text, not an email, so it has no subject line.

## Recruitment email

**No callout version:**

1. You're in! BinaryHeart's first meeting is Thursday, Oct 8
2. {{First Name}}, come take apart a laptop with us on Oct 8
3. BinaryHeart's first meeting: Thursday, Oct 8, drop in anytime 11-6

**Callout version:**

1. You're in! Callout meeting + first meeting details
2. {{First Name}}, here's how to get started with BinaryHeart this fall
3. BinaryHeart at NU: callout meeting and first meeting details
