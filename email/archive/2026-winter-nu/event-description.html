What to Expect

BinaryHeart is a student organization at universities and high schools in the US that refurbishes “retired” technology for donation to underserved groups. This meeting will start with a presentation for new members to provide more information on BinaryHeart's mission, history, and future plans. Then, you'll get hands-on experience learning computer repair skills to refurbish devices for donation to underserved groups.

Regular Meeting Schedule

For the rest of the quarter, we will be holding meetings on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday every week from 3:00 - 5:30 PM.

You can attend as many or as few meetings as you would like. Meetings are walk-in and there is no fixed time commitment.

Since everyone's schedule is different, we will be onboarding people at meetings both this week and next, so don't worry if you aren't able to attend this week!
