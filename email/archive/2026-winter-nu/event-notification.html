Computer Repair Workshop

First Meeting Details

When

Friday, October 10th
11:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Come anytime during these hours!)

Where

Our BinaryHeart Space:
1223 Hull Terrace in Evanston ("Evanston South")

What We'll Do

We'll be teaching you how to repair computers so that you'll be able to refurbish devices for donation to underserved groups. This is a hands-on learning experience where you'll gain practical skills while making a positive impact in our community.

Getting There

Our space is about a 10-minute drive from campus. Here are three ways to get there:

  1. BH Ride: Message our Instagram/Slack or email nu@binaryheart.org before or during our open hours.
    • Our regular pick-up spot is at our satellite office ("Evanston North"): 1910 Orrington Ave in Evanston (directly across from Foster, 1 block from the arch)
    • If you need us to pick you up somewhere else (e.g. Tech), let us know and we will make that happen!
  2. Uber/Lyft: We will pay for Ubers/Lyfts to the space if we are not able to drive you. Message our chapter's Instagram/Slack or email nu@binaryheart.org and we can either:
    • Order you an Uber, OR;
    • Reimburse you: Order one with your account and send a receipt to nu@binaryheart.org along with either your Venmo or Zelle and we will reimburse you within 24 hours.
  3. Public Transportation - 2 options:
    • Bus: Take the 201 Line to the Ridge & Hull Terrace stop and walk 5 minutes (2 blocks) to the other end of Hull Terrace.
      View CTA 201 Map OR;
    • Train: Take the Purple Line to South Boulevard. Message our chapter's Instagram/Slack account or email us and a club leader will pick you up (or we will get you an Uber - the walk is about 25 minutes but drive is about 4 minutes)
