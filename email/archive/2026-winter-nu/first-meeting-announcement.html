Getting There

Our space is about a 10-minute drive from campus. Here are three ways to get there:

  1. BH Ride: Message our Instagram/Slack or email nu@binaryheart.org before or during our open hours.
    • Regular pick-up spot: 1910 Orrington Ave (across from Foster, 1 block from the Arch)
    • If you need pickup elsewhere (e.g., Tech), let us know and we’ll make it happen.
  2. Uber/Lyft: If we’re not able to drive you, we will pay for rides to the space. DM our Instagram/Slack or email nu@binaryheart.org and we can either:
    • Order you an Uber, OR
    • Reimburse you: Use your account and send the receipt to nu@binaryheart.org with your Venmo or Zelle for reimbursement within 24 hours.
  3. Public Transportation – two options:
    • Bus: Take the 201 Line to the Ridge & Hull Terrace stop and walk ~5 minutes (2 blocks) to the other end of Hull Terrace. Click the button below to view a simple map showing all of the stops on campus on the 201 Line and where to get off.
      View CTA 201 Map
    • Train: Take the Purple Line to South Boulevard, then DM our Instagram/Slack or email us and a leader will pick you up (or we’ll get you an Uber). The walk from the station is ~25 minutes; the drive is ~4 minutes.
