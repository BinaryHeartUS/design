Getting Involved

Volunteering Hours

We work on an "open house system" where hours are completely flexible. You can attend as much or as little as you would like each week within our open hours. These are our general hours for Winter Quarter:

  • Mondays: 3:00 - 5:30 PM
  • Wednesdays: 3:00 - 5:30 PM
  • Fridays: 3:00 - 5:30 PM

You can attend as many or as few meetings as you would like. Meetings are walk-in and there is no fixed time commitment.

Space Location

We meet at our space: 1910 Orrington Ave, directly across the street from Foster and about a 4-5 minute walk from the Arch.

The entrance to our space is on the right side of the house.

If you need any help getting to the space, message our Instagram (@binaryheartatnu) or email nu@binaryheart.org.

Important:

Make sure to use your school email address for both accounts.

Apps Setup

Notion Setup

To get started with Notion:

  1. Click this link to join: binaryhe.art/joinNotion
  2. Sign up using your Northwestern email address
  3. Download Notion on both your computer and phone: binaryhe.art/downloadNotion

Slack Setup

To get started with Slack:

  1. Click this link to join: binaryhe.art/joinSlack
  2. Sign up using your Northwestern email address
  3. Download Slack on both your computer and phone: binaryhe.art/downloadSlack

Complete Your Setup

Final steps:

  1. Log into both Notion and Slack on your computer and phone
  2. Complete your profile setup in both platforms and ensure notifications are enabled on all of your devices
  3. Message and/or email the person helping you with onboarding (or the person CCed on this email) to set up an onboarding meeting

Congratulations! You should be all set with your BinaryHeart logins.

Let us know once you've completed these steps or if you run into any issues or questions.
