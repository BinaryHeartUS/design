Logistics

We will be meeting at our primary space: 1223 Hull Terrace in Evanston ("Evanston South"). We work on an "open house system", where hours are completely flexible. You can attend as much or as little as you would like each week within the hours that we are open. For Fall quarter, our open volunteering/refurbishment hours will be:

  • Tuesdays: 4-6 PM → Non-Exam Weeks
  • Wednesdays: 4-6 PM → Non-Exam Weeks
  • Fridays: 3-5 PM → All Weeks

Please follow our Instagram, @binaryheartatnu, in order to get weekly updates on what our schedule will be. We will increase hours on Fridays if people are unable to make these times, please DM the Instagram account or email nu@binaryheart.org and we can be at the space earlier or later in the day.

Transportation

Our space is about a 10-minute drive from Campus. There are three ways you can get there:

  1. BH Ride: Message our Instagram/Slack or email nu@binaryheart.org before or during our open hours.
    • Our regular pick-up spot is at our satellite office: 1910 Orrington Ave in Evanston (directly across from Foster, 1 block from the arch)
    • If you need us to pick you up somewhere else (e.g. Tech), let us know and we will make that happen!
  2. Uber/Lyft: We will pay for Ubers/Lyfts to the space if we are not able to drive you. Message our chapter's Instagram/Slack or email nu@binaryheart.org and we can either:
    • Order you an Uber, OR;
    • Reimburse you: Order one with your account and send a receipt to nu@binaryheart.org along with either your Venmo or Zelle and we will reimburse you within 24 hours.
  3. Public Transportation - 2 options:
    • Bus: Take the 201 Line from the Chicago and Clark stop to the Ridge & Hull Terrace stop and walk 5 minutes (2 blocks) to the other end of Hull Terrace, OR;
    • Train: Take the Purple Line to South Boulevard. Message our chapter's Instagram/Slack account or email us and a club leader will pick you up (or we will get you an Uber - the walk is about 25 minutes but drive is about 4 minutes)

First Meeting and Training

Regular club meetings will start on Friday, October 10th at Evanston South (1223 Hull Ter). We are planning to be around the space for most of that day to get an idea of when people are available, so feel free to come any time between 11 to 5 PM.

We will provide an in-depth training to teach you how to disassemble, diagnose, repair, upgrade, and reassemble devices. No prior technical experience or weekly time commitment will ever be required to be a part of Refurbishment!
